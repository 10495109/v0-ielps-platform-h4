How IELPS helps you

Learning that adapts to you as you go

As you learn, IELPS works quietly in the background — shaping every activity around where you are and where you're heading next.

  • Adapt activities to your level and performance

  • Help you practise vocabulary, grammar, listening, reading, speaking and writing

  • Identify the skills that need more practice

  • Bring important learning back through Smart Review

  • Give you immediate feedback and support

  • Track your progress as you move towards your next CEFR level

There is no need to rush and no need to follow anyone else's pace.

Adult learner flow

From My Course to the correct adult lesson player

Adult scholars continue from their dashboard straight into the authoritative integrated lesson player — no detours, no duplicate routes.

  1. 01

    My Course

    /dashboard

    Loads course state and the recommended next lesson.

  2. 02

    Continue Lesson

    CTA

    The continue action resolves the next eligible lesson ID.

  3. 03

    Lesson Player

    /learner?lesson=:id

    Opens IELPS-A1-C2 Lesson Player — Integrated Final.

  4. 04

    Completion

    Saves progress, then returns to course, Discover or StarPath.
